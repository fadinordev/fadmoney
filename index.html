FadMoney Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · App version 1.1.0 · Package com.fadmoney.app

1. Who we are

FadMoney (the “app”) is a personal finance tracker for Android published by the developer FadinorDev (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. Contact: fadinordev@gmail.com.

Summary: we collect nothing.

FadMoney has no accounts, no registration and no sign-in. It contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We do not receive, store, sell or share any of your personal or financial data.

2. Data stored on your device

FadMoney is an offline app. The information you enter — accounts and account groups, expenses, income, transfers, categories and subcategories, notes, amounts, currencies, exchange rates and your settings — is stored only in a local database on your device. We have no access to it and it is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are disabled in the app, so this data is not copied to your Google account by the app.

3. Network requests

The app uses the INTERNET permission only to download public currency exchange rates (for UAH, USD and EUR). These are plain GET requests that contain no personal data, no financial data and no identifiers from the app. The rates are requested from:

  • open.er-api.com (open exchange-rate API);
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net (the “currency-api” dataset by fawazahmed0, delivered through jsDelivr);
  • latest.currency-api.pages.dev (mirror of the same dataset);
  • bank.gov.ua (the National Bank of Ukraine).

As with any web request, these servers can see the IP address of your device and the time of the request. The app sends nothing else. You can use FadMoney fully offline with the bundled rates or by setting rates manually.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET — only to download public exchange rates, as described above.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC — to let you unlock the app with a fingerprint or face. Biometric data is processed entirely by the Android system; the app only receives a “success” or “failure” result and never sees or stores your biometrics.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, SMS or any other personal data.

5. Backups

You can manually export your data to a .fad backup file and import it later. The file is saved to a location you choose with the Android system file picker. The app does not upload the backup anywhere. The file is not encrypted, so you are responsible for where you keep it and with whom you share it.

6. Security (PIN and biometrics)

You can protect the app with a PIN and/or biometric unlock. The PIN is stored on the device only as a salted hash, never in plain text, and is never sent anywhere. Your data stays in the app’s private storage on your device; protecting the device itself (screen lock, updates) remains your responsibility.

7. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries. The only external services involved are the public exchange-rate providers listed in section 3 (open.er-api.com, jsDelivr / fawazahmed0 currency-api, currency-api.pages.dev and the National Bank of Ukraine); they receive only the technical information inherent to any web request, such as your IP address, and are governed by their own policies.

8. Children

FadMoney is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

9. Data deletion and retention

We hold no copies of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete individual items inside the app, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → FadMoney → Storage, or uninstall the app; any of these removes the data. Backup .fad files you exported are separate files under your control and must be deleted by you.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app’s features change. The current version is always available at this page, and the effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If a change affects how data is handled, we will update the policy before the new behavior is released.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to FadinorDev: fadinordev@gmail.com.
